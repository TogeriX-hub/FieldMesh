Browser not supported. Flashing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux). Phones and Safari do not work.

01 · CHOOSE YOUR DEVICE

02 · FLASH

Connect the device to this computer with a USB data cable. Settings, contacts and identity stay on the device – nothing is erased.
  1. Click Flash and pick the device's port.
  2. Wait until it says done. The device restarts by itself.
  1. Click Start update mode and pick the device's port. The device restarts into update mode.
  2. Click Flash and pick the port again – its name may have changed.
Already in update mode? Go straight to Flash. If Start update mode does nothing, put the device into update (DFU) mode by hand – see your device's manual – and then click Flash.
